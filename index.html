A Warm Sanctuary Built On Good Food & Happy Vibes

Welcome to Hency Cafe, where our foundational motto is simple yet heartfelt: GOOD FOOD • GOOD MOOD • GOOD DAY. We started with an ambition to create a space that feels like a natural extension of your own living room — cozy, cheerful, and filled with intoxicating aromas.

Whether you need a quick morning cup of authentic tea for just ₹15, a college break over bubbling spicy Maggi and crispy peri peri fries, or an evening pizza hangout with friends, Hency Cafe is designed to be your favorite neighborhood refuge.
